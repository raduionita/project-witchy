Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QVONYX ("we", "us", "our") handles your information in the Witchy mobile application ("Witchy", "the App"). It also explains the choices and rights you have. By using Witchy you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

Your health data stays on your device. Witchy stores everything you log locally on your phone or tablet. We do not upload it to our servers, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or analytics. Witchy works without an account.

2. Who We Are and How to Reach Us

Controller: QVONYX. Service: Witchy. Privacy contact: witchy@qvonyx.com. We do not have a formal data protection officer; you can reach us at the same address for any privacy request.

3. Information We Handle

All of the following is stored locally in the App on your device (application preferences). Nothing in this list is transmitted to us:

  • Cycle and period data: recorded period dates, cycle phases, and related calendar entries used to calculate predictions.
  • Daily logs (health data): symptoms, mood, flow, sexual activity, notes, and similar entries you record for a given day.
  • Profile: year of birth and any profile details you choose to enter.
  • Account details (optional): if you sign in with Google or Apple, a limited profile (display name and email or equivalent identifier) is stored locally so the App can keep you signed in. You can use Witchy without an account.
  • Settings and reminders: notification preferences, onboarding choices, consent records, and reminder settings you configure.

Information you enter about your cycle, symptoms, and sexual health is sensitive health data (including special-category data under the GDPR). We process it only with your consent, to provide the features you asked for.

4. What We Do Not Do

  • No advertising networks or trackers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No selling, renting, or profiling of your data.
  • No location tracking.
  • No sharing of health data with third parties.
  • No servers receiving your logs: we have no backend that stores your cycle information.

5. How and Why We Use Information

  • To provide the App (store your logs, calculate predictions, show insights): legal basis - your consent, given when you accept this policy.
  • Reminders you enable (for example, period or pill reminders): your consent; you can turn them off at any time in device or App settings.
  • Sign-in (optional) - to keep you signed in through Google or Apple: your consent, revocable by signing out.
  • To run and improve the App - limited to maintenance and debugging performed on your device; we do not receive your data for this.
  • Legal obligations - only if we are legally required to retain or disclose information.

6. Third Parties

We do not share your health data with anyone. The App does interact with a few services for specific, limited purposes:

  • Google Sign-In and Apple Sign-In (optional): used only if you choose to sign in. These providers receive only what is needed to authenticate you, under their own privacy policies. We confirm that they provide the same or equal protection of your data as described in this policy, as required by Apple's App Store Review Guidelines.
  • Google Fonts: the App's typography may be loaded from Google Fonts, which processes your device's IP address to deliver the font files (Google's privacy policy).
  • In-app browser: legal pages, including this policy, open in an in-app browser view served from witchy.qvonyx.com. The web server may process standard technical logs (such as IP address) for security.

We do not allow any third party to use your data for their own advertising, marketing, or data mining.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data is stored on your device, retention is under your control:

  • While you use the App: data stays until you delete it, from within the App where a delete option exists, or by clearing the App's data.
  • When you uninstall the App or clear its data: locally stored information is removed from the device (operating-system behavior may vary).
  • If you signed in: you can sign out at any time. To request deletion of any account-related information we hold, email witchy@qvonyx.com and we will delete it within 30 days, except where we are legally required to keep it.
  • Consent records: your acceptance of this policy is stored locally as a consent timestamp.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA, or similar laws), you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you (we will respond within 30 days);
  • correct inaccurate data - most of it you can edit directly in the App;
  • erase your data (uninstall or clear the App, or email us for account data);
  • port your data (export features in the App where available, or email us);
  • withdraw consent at any time - withdrawal does not affect processing that already happened;
  • restrict or object to processing, and lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

To exercise any right, email witchy@qvonyx.com. We may need to verify your identity first. We do not discriminate against users who exercise these rights.

9. Children

Witchy is for people aged 16 and over. The App is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a person under 16 has provided personal data through the App, contact witchy@qvonyx.com and we will delete it. We do not knowingly permit accounts for users under 16 and we do not request age information beyond the year of birth you choose to enter.

10. Security

Because your data lives on your own device, its security depends on your device security: we recommend using a device passcode, biometric lock, and keeping your operating system up to date. We apply reasonable measures to protect information we do handle (such as the local consent record) and any messages sent to our privacy contact.

11. International Transfers

Witchy stores data on your device in your country. Optional sign-in and web services (Google, Apple, Google Fonts, witchy.qvonyx.com) may process information in other countries; where safeguards are required for transfers out of the EEA or UK, those providers rely on standard contractual clauses or adequacy decisions under their own policies.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The "Last updated" date shows when it changed. Material changes will be shown in the App and, where required, we will ask you to consent again. Continued use after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

13. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: witchy@qvonyx.com
