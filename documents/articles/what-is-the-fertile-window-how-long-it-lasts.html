What Is the Fertile Window? How Long It Lasts

How long is the fertile window?

The fertile window is about six days: the five days before ovulation plus the day of ovulation. These are the days when sex is most likely to lead to pregnancy.

Why these days matter

An egg survives only about 12 to 24 hours after release. Sperm can live in the reproductive tract for three to five days, so sex in the days before ovulation can still lead to fertilisation once the egg is released. Sex after ovulation has a much smaller window.

Why app predictions are estimates

Apps predict your fertile window from past cycles, but ovulation can shift from month to month because of stress, illness, travel or changes in routine. The follicular phase is the part of the cycle most likely to vary. Predictions are a starting point, not a guarantee.

Tracking that can sharpen your timing

  • Cervical mucus: Clear, stretchy, egg-white mucus signals peak fertility.
  • Ovulation predictor kits: These detect the LH surge in urine, usually starting around day 10 and used each morning until a positive result.
  • Basal body temperature: A small, sustained rise after ovulation confirms it has happened, though it is best used alongside other signs.

When to get help

If you have been trying for 12 months without success, or six months if you are 35 or older, speak to a doctor. Seek help sooner if your cycles are very irregular or you have known fertility concerns.
