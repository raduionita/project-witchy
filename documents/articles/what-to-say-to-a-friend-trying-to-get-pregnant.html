What to Say to a Friend Trying to Get Pregnant

What should you say to someone trying to conceive?

When a friend is trying to conceive, the most helpful thing is usually to listen, follow their lead and stay present. You don't need perfect words.

Supportive things to say

  • "Thanks for telling me. How are you feeling about it?"
  • "I'm here if you want to talk, and I'm happy to talk about other things too."
  • "Is there anything you'd like me to do or not do right now?"
  • "That sounds really hard. I'm sorry you're going through it."

Things to avoid

  • "Just relax, it'll happen." Pressure to relax can feel dismissive.
  • Asking for updates on their cycle or timing.
  • Sharing stories of people who conceived quickly, or offering unrequested advice.
  • Commenting on their body, weight or diet as the reason it isn't working.

Follow their lead

Some friends want to talk about it every day. Others want it to be a topic they never raise. Ask what helps, and let them change their mind. Check in regularly without expecting a full update each time.

Be there whatever the outcome

If they become pregnant, they may still feel mixed emotions. If they go through loss, or fertility treatment is needed, your steady presence matters. Acknowledge their experience and avoid making the conversation about yourself.
