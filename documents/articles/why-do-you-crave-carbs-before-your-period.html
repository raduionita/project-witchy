Why Do You Crave Carbs Before Your Period?

Why do I crave carbs before my period?

Appetite and cravings can rise in the luteal phase, the one or two weeks before a period, and they tend to be stronger for people who have premenstrual syndrome (PMS). Cravings are common, and they are not a personal failing.

What the research shows

A 2025 study of 150 women in the Journal of Nutritional Science found that PMS affected "hedonic hunger," which is the urge to eat for pleasure when the body doesn't need energy. Other research in the same area reports that food cravings are very common in menstruating women, and that the foods people crave are often energy-dense and fatty. Carbohydrate-rich foods are often part of the picture, but the evidence is about cravings in general, not a single food.

Why it may happen

Hormones shift during the luteal phase, and PMS can affect mood, sleep and appetite. Feeling tired or low can make comfort foods more appealing. None of this means you lack willpower.

What may help you feel steadier

  • Eat regular meals and snacks so you don't go long stretches hungry.
  • Pair carbohydrates with protein or fibre, such as wholegrain pasta with beans or vegetables.
  • Get enough sleep, since tiredness tends to increase cravings.
  • Allow yourself the foods you want. Restriction often makes cravings stronger.

When to talk to a doctor

The NHS advises seeing a GP if PMS symptoms are affecting your daily life or if lifestyle changes have not helped. Speak to a doctor sooner if cravings feel out of control, if you binge eat, or if your mood is very low.
