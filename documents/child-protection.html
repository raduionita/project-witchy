Safe Space and Child Protection

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This page explains the age rules for Witchy ("the App"), operated by QVONYX, and how we protect younger users. It forms part of our legal documents together with the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy.

1. Minimum Age: 16

You must be at least 16 years old to use Witchy.

When you accept the consent screen in the App, you confirm that you are of the required age. If you are under 16, do not use Witchy.

We set this minimum because Witchy handles sensitive reproductive health information, and because people under 16 cannot give valid consent to the processing of such data under laws like the GDPR without parental permission. We keep one clear rule instead of different ages by region.

2. The App Is Not Directed at Children Under 16

  • Witchy is not designed, marketed, or intended for children under 16.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16.
  • We do not condition participation in any activity on disclosing more personal information than is reasonably necessary.
  • Content in the App (cycle, symptom, and reproductive health information) is written for users aged 16 and over.

3. If an Under-16 User Uses the App

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a person under 16 has used Witchy, or if you are under 16 and want your data removed, email witchy@qvonyx.com. Tell us what happened and we will delete the relevant data and close any associated access, within 30 days.

Because Witchy stores data locally on the device, the fastest removal is to delete the App or clear its App data from the device settings.

4. No Accounts for Children

Sign-in with Google or Apple is optional and only for users aged 16 or over. We do not knowingly create or maintain accounts for people under 16. We do not collect children's contact details, device identifiers for tracking, or precise location, and we do not run advertising or analytics in the App, so no data about young users is sent to third parties.

5. Safe Space Standards

To keep Witchy a supportive place, users must not:

  • share content that is harmful, abusive, or harassing to others;
  • post or encourage medical misinformation or dangerous health advice;
  • attempt to identify, contact, or harm other users;
  • use the App for any unlawful purpose.

Witchy is a personal tracking tool, not a community or social platform, and it does not host user content on shared servers.

6. Health and Safety Warnings

  • Witchy is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition.
  • Predictions are estimates, not medical facts. Do not use them as contraception or as a substitute for professional care.
  • Always consult a doctor or qualified healthcare professional about your health and before making medical decisions.
  • Young users should use Witchy together with guidance from a parent, guardian, or healthcare professional.

7. Questions and Reports

Safety concerns, age questions, or deletion requests: witchy@qvonyx.com
