Privacy first

Your health data never leaves your device

Witchy is built around your privacy, not analytics. What you log about your body is yours, stored locally in the app, and nothing more.

  • No servers holding your logs. Cycle and symptom data stay on your phone or tablet.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs. Nothing is sold, rented, or used to profile you.
  • No location tracking. We never ask for, and never receive, where you are.
  • No account needed. Sign in with Google or Apple only if you want sync.
  • One-time consent. You accept the policies once, stored locally as a timestamp.

Questions

Before you download

Is Witchy free?

You can download Witchy and start tracking right away. Any optional purchases are clearly marked in the app - nothing is hidden behind the tracking features themselves.

Do I need an account?

No. Choose "Skip for now" on the join screen and you can use everything locally. Signing in with Google or Apple is optional and exists only if you want your settings to follow you across devices.

Is my cycle data uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything you log is stored in the app's local preferences on your device. We have no backend that receives your cycle, symptom, or sexual health information. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.

Does Witchy contain trackers or analytics?

None. There are no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, no profiling, and no location tracking anywhere in the app.

How old do I have to be?

You must be at least 16 years old to use Witchy, because it handles sensitive reproductive health information. Details are in the Safe Space and Child Protection notice.

Can I use Witchy for pregnancy or fertility planning?

Yes - there is a pregnancy tracker with week-by-week guidance and a fertility window view with charts. These are informational estimates and should not replace professional medical advice or be relied on as contraception.

How do I delete my data?

Your data lives on your device, so removing it is in your hands: clear the app's data or uninstall the app. If you signed in and want account-related information deleted, email witchy@qvonyx.com and we will act within 30 days.
