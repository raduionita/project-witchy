# Algorithm deep dive
Checked by hand: the gaps between your dates are 23, 23, 27, 26, 26, 27 days. The mean is 25.3, the median 26, and the range 23–27. The cycle day (19 on Oct 20) and the logged days are correct.
How Witchy's numbers are derived:
• Onboarding slider, Insights average and the next-period date (Oct 28) all equal 26, the median.
• Profile shows 25, the mean.
• The range Oct 25–29 is the shortest and longest cycle.
• Ovulation is cycle length minus 14, which gives day 12.
• The window is ovulation −5 to +1, which gives days 7–13.
• All of this is internally consistent except the mean versus median split.
Where Witchy stands against Flo, Clue and Stardust:
:red_circle: High
• Mean vs median. Choose one method. I'd suggest a median or weighted average of the last 3 to 6 cycles. Flo and Clue base predictions on recent cycles and show one value.
• Range uses min and max of all cycles. A single 49-day cycle would widen the range to 5–29 days. Exclude outliers or use ±1 standard deviation over the last 6 cycles. Clue and Flo show a shaded range around the predicted period.
• Fixed 14-day luteal phase and a 7-day window, regardless of variability. Widen the window when the cycle spread is high. Flo and Clue let users log ovulation tests, basal temperature and cervical fluid to correct ovulation. Witchy's "TTC Tips" tell users to track these, but the app has no input for them. Add the inputs, or label the ovulation date "estimated".
:large_yellow_circle: Medium
• No confidence indicator. Add "Based on 6 cycles" under the predictions. Both apps say predictions improve as more cycles are logged.
• Labels and thresholds. A 23-day cycle is in the commonly cited normal range of about 21–35 days. Suggested thresholds are Short under 21, Long over 35, and Irregular when the spread is over 7 days. If cycles stay outside normal, show a soft "consider talking to a doctor" note.
• Late period. I can't see this in the screenshots. After Oct 28 passes without a log, does the cycle day keep counting and does the prediction shift? Test it. Flo and Clue handle "period late" explicitly.
• Logged data vs onboarding. Real logs should override inferred ones. Average bleed (5.0 days across 7 periods) is currently just the default, so calculate it only from logged start and end dates.
• Perimenopause and Pregnancy modes. Does the algorithm change at all? If not, hide them or label them "coming soon".
:large_green_circle: Low
• Stardust doesn't publish its algorithm details. Its positioning is on-device privacy plus an astrology layer, and Witchy already matches the privacy side. A moon-phase layer could be a differentiator.
:warning: I can't browse in this chat, so the Flo, Clue and Stardust points come from general knowledge of their public descriptions. Verify them before using them in a decision. The Witchy numbers above are calculated from your screenshots.
